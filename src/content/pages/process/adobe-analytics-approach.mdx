## How a digital analytics implementation fits together

This site shows how I plan, build and look after a digital analytics implementation, worked through on two demo sites that run real tags.

Start with the map below. Business requirements become one design document, and four streams of work follow from it.

Select any box to see that step done on this site.

<ImplementationMap colorBy="stream" table={false} />

## What the map shows

- The top row is the starting point: what the business needs to know, and the solution design reference (SDR) that turns each need into variables.
- Each coloured band is a stream of work: setting up the analytics platform, setting up the pages and tags, writing the procedures and documentation, and keeping it all correct after launch.
- Arrows show order. The first two streams are sequences, and the platform comes before the tags. The last two are sets of deliverables.
