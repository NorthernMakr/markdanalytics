**Every variable starts as a question someone needs answered.** This document records those questions before anyone thinks about eVars or GA4 parameters. Each requirement names the decision it will inform, so a request without a decision ("track everything, just in case") has nowhere to go.

## How the requirements were gathered

Requirements came from three groups of stakeholders, each asked different questions.

- **Business leaders and product owners** (sometimes the same person). A short survey first, then a face-to-face meeting to follow up. The questions: their KPIs and targets, which metrics have proven valuable in the past and why, what their ideal dashboard would show, and any roadmaps or mission statements the measurement should support.
- **Technology leaders.** An interview to bring out technical constraints and the changes planned for the near to medium term. It's also a chance to find out what digital analytics could do for them: questions about how people reach the site (web or mobile, Apple or Android), screen load times and so on.
- **Other stakeholders, such as legal and privacy.** A conversation about what may be collected, and on what conditions. This is where the one constraint recorded with the requirements comes from: no personal information in analytics.

Each answer was then rewritten as a question, with the decision it informs and the KPIs that would answer it. Priorities were agreed with the digital lead: **High** = needed for launch reporting, **Medium** = needed within a quarter.

## Out of scope

- Logged-in behaviour (neither site has accounts).
- Marketing channel attribution (campaign parameters are collected by default; attribution models are an analysis question, not an implementation one).
- Personal information of any kind (BR-10).
