**Every variable starts as a question someone needs answered.** This document records those questions before anyone thinks about eVars or GA4 parameters. Each requirement names the decision it will inform, so a request without a decision ("track everything, just in case") has nowhere to go.

## How the requirements were gathered

A short interview was held with each owner: *what do you decide, how often, and what would change your mind?* The person leading these interviews should keep in mind that business owners and technical leads may not know everything analytics can do. Requirements gathering is also a conversation: it educates and explores what is possible.

Each answer was then turned into a single business question, paired with the decision or topic it informs and the KPIs that support it. Priorities were set with the digital lead and product owners: **High** means required for launch reporting; **Medium** means required within three months of launch.

One constraint was recorded alongside the questions: no personal information in analytics.

## Out of scope

- Logged-in behaviour (neither site has accounts).
- Marketing channel attribution (campaign parameters are collected by default; attribution models are an analysis question, not an implementation one).
- Personal information of any kind (BR-10).
