**Every variable starts as a question someone needs answered.** This document records those questions before anyone thinks about eVars or GA4 parameters. Each requirement names the decision it will inform, so a request without a decision ("track everything, just in case") has nowhere to go.

## How the requirements were gathered

- A short interview with each owner: *what do you decide, how often, and what would change your mind?*
- Each answer rewritten as one question, with the decision and the KPIs that would answer it.
- Priorities agreed with the digital lead: **High** = needed for launch reporting, **Medium** = needed within a quarter.
- One constraint recorded alongside the questions: no personal information in analytics.

## The requirements

The last column is filled in from the [solution design reference](/implementation/sdr/), so every requirement traces to the variables that answer it, and every variable traces back to a requirement.

## Out of scope

- Logged-in behaviour (neither site has accounts).
- Marketing channel attribution (campaign parameters are collected by default; attribution models are an analysis question, not an implementation one).
- Personal information of any kind (BR-10).
