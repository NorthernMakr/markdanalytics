I'm Mark Dykeman, a digital analytics specialist in Victoria, British Columbia. I work Adobe Analytics first and Google Analytics second, across the whole chain: requirements, solution design, implementation specs, data quality and analysis. This site is where I show that work.

I work in English and French, and have basic German and Japanese.

## Résumé

Digital analytics practitioner with hands-on experience in loyalty, banking, media and agency settings. I've taken part in and led three major analytics implementations, at CBC, BMO and AIR MILES, and built the business cases for Adobe Analytics at BMO and AIR MILES and for Adobe Target at BMO.

### What I do

- **Measurement design:** KPI definition, requirements, solution design documents and tagging specifications
- **Implementation:** Adobe Analytics, Adobe Target and Adobe Launch; Google Tag Manager administration; data layer requirements
- **Data quality:** QA processes and debugging on web and mobile, with browser network tools, Omnibug, BrowserStack and Adobe Experience Platform Assurance
- **Analysis:** behaviour and conversion analysis, segmentation, A/B testing, dashboards in Analysis Workspace
- **Practice building:** business cases, vendor evaluation, training, Agile delivery
- **Tools I'm comfortable with:** Databricks (metric views and Genie), Jira and Confluence, and AI assistants including ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
